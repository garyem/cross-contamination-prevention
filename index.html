Driver Cross-Contamination Safety Checklist

Your daily guide to safe mobility equipment delivery and collection.

Today's shift progress0 / 0

1

Start of Shift: Unload Collections

Before anything else

  • Unload previous collections
    Transfer any equipment still in the vehicle from the last round straight to the depot's isolated decontamination holding bay before you prep for the day.
  • Return undelivered items to stock
    Any items not delivered on the last round are to be returned to stock.
2

Sanitise & Prepare Vehicle

Once the vehicle is empty

  • Sanitise the cargo hold
    Wipe down anchoring rails, load-securing points and floor beds with approved disinfectant.
  • Set up Clean Zone / Dirty Zone separation
    Keep a clear physical barrier or safe clearance between the forward "clean" area and the rearward "dirty" collection area — a standard infection-control practice.
  • Stock your PPE
    Confirm a full day's supply of nitrile gloves, aprons, hand sanitiser and clinical waste bags.
  • Inspect outgoing stock
    Check every outgoing item is sealed in intact, undamaged plastic wrapping.
3

Delivery Protocol

At the service user's home

  • Keep it sealed until arrival
    Transport the equipment in its plastic wrap right up to the threshold before opening.
  • Don fresh PPE
    Sanitise hands and put on a new pair of gloves immediately before handling the clean device indoors.
  • Unwrap in front of the service user
    Open the packaging visibly so they can see the item arrives clean.
  • Strip PPE and sanitise
    Peel gloves off inside-out, bin them in your waste pouch, then sanitise hands before returning to the cab.
4

Collection Protocol

Picking up returned equipment

  • Pre-inspect the item
    Check for sharp breakages, structural failure or visible biological matter before moving it.
  • Bag it at the point of collection
    Enclose the item fully in a heavy-duty, colour-coded transit sleeve before carrying it through the home.
  • Load to the Dirty Zone
    Secure it in the rear Dirty Zone using only the dedicated dirty-side tie-down straps.
  • Remove PPE straight after
    Bin gloves and apron in the vehicle's waste bin. Never touch the steering wheel with soiled PPE.
Note: colour-coded bagging follows the same segregation principle used across NHS healthcare waste handling — keeping contaminated items visually and physically separate at every stage.
5

Depot Unloading

End of route — closes the loop

  • Direct offload
    Transfer all collected items straight to the depot's isolated decontamination holding bay.
  • Log any incidents
    Report immediately if a clean seal was compromised or a fluid leak occurred in transit.
  • Wash down the vehicle
    Sanitise all Dirty Zone surfaces with medical-grade disinfectant, ready for the next round or shift.
